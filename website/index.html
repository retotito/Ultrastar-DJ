Made for karaoke nights — in bars, at parties, at home.

What it can do

3 screens

Your console plus two game displays

Put two singers on each TV or projector, or all four on one. Every display keeps its place and size for the next party. Only one screen? The game then plays right in the DJ window.

Preview on headphones

Listen in before the crowd hears it

The preview player has its own audio output. Check the next song on your headphones while the game plays on the big speakers.

Songbook

Guests request songs from their phones

No app to install. Guests scan the code, search all your songs and send a request — over Wi-Fi or mobile data, with an optional party PIN.

  1. Start the songbook
  2. Guests scan the code on the screen
  3. They search and request
  4. The request appears next to your queue
Duets

Duet songs supported

Songs written as duets (marked [DUET]) give each voice its own part. Choose who sings which voice, or let one singer take both.

YouTube or your own files

A .txt and a YouTube link — that's a song

Notes and lyrics come from a small UltraStar text file. If it has a YouTube link, music and video play straight from YouTube — no files to collect.

Classic UltraStar songs work too: MP3 or other audio files, with or without a video (MP4, MKV, AVI and more).

Ready in seven steps

Set up once — the app remembers everything for the next party.

  1. Choose your songs

    Add your UltraStar folders, or sign in to USDB.

  2. Pick the speakers

    One output for the game, one for your headphones.

  3. Give each singer a microphone

    Assign a microphone to each player, then press Calibrate to measure its delay with the game speakers — so every note is scored fairly, even over Bluetooth.

  4. Put the singers on screens

    Choose who sings on which display, open it and drag the window onto the TV or projector, then Fullscreen.

  5. Preview

    Listen to a song on your headphones before you play it.

  6. Line them up

    Queue songs, or take the guests' requests.

  7. Play

    Load the song into the Game Player and press play — 3, 2, 1, sing.

Your download has started

The app is not signed by Apple or Microsoft (that costs a yearly fee), so your computer asks once whether you trust it. Here is how to say yes:

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Ultrastar DJ into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS says it cannot check the app — click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. Confirm with your password. From now on it opens normally.
  1. Start UltrastarDJ-win-Setup.exe.
  2. A blue window says "Windows protected your PC" — click More info.
  3. Click Run anyway. The app installs and starts by itself.
  1. Install mpv once: sudo apt install libmpv2 (on older systems libmpv1).
  2. Right-click UltrastarDJ.AppImage → Properties → Permissions → Allow executing file as program.
  3. Double-click it.

Updates after that install themselves — no more questions.

Create your own songs

A few free tools to write your own UltraStar songs — notes, lyrics and timing in one .txt file that Ultrastar DJ plays like any other.

  • Ultrastar Creator Tool Upload a song and get a singable first draft: the voice is separated, lyrics aligned and pitches detected — then fine-tune it in a piano-roll editor.
  • Yass Reloaded Karaoke editor for fine-tuning notes, lyrics and timing, and for tidying up a whole song library. Windows, Mac, Linux.
  • UltraStar-Creator The UltraStar Deluxe team's song creation tool. Windows, Mac, Linux.
